# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is an open, welcoming community. Everyone who participates — contributors,
maintainers, and people opening issues or discussions — is expected to help keep it a
respectful and harassment-free environment, regardless of background, identity, or level of
experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in technical disagreement; critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and acknowledge mistakes.
- Keep discussion focused on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting is not
tolerated. This includes harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing
others' private information without permission, and any unwelcome attention directed at another
participant. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this Code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any participant for behavior they judge
harmful.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, and
discussions — and whenever an individual represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer at **personal@yurii-anichkin.dev**.
All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and
safety are respected. Maintainers are obligated to keep the details of any report confidential.
